# Contributing

Open the matching [issue form](https://github.com/tld-wiki/community/issues/new/choose). The **Suggest an edit** link on a product page fills in that page for you.

- Describe one focused correction or addition.
- Link the official source supporting factual claims. Distinguish verified facts from unknowns.
- For translations, include the page or message, language and proposed wording. Keep placeholders such as `{count}` unchanged.
- Write in your own words. Identify adapted wording, its creator and source, relevant permission or license, and material AI assistance. Do not invent attribution or claim checks you did not perform.
- Keep credentials, cookies, private account details and personal registration data out of public issues.

GitHub identifies the submitter. Maintainers may request clarification, verify source evidence, accept or reject the suggestion, and close duplicates or spam. An accepted issue is a research/review decision; only a separately reviewed knowledge or application release changes the product. Published revision history records actual committed editorial changes.

This intake does not grant access to private repositories, confer rights to third-party content, or transfer application ownership. Reuse and contribution terms for any future knowledge publication remain a separate owner decision.
